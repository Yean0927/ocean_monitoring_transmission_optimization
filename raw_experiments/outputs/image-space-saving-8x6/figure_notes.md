# Image space saving, 8 × 6 inches

Contract: single-panel quantitative comparison of reported space saving for
eight image-processing methods. All eight percentages and method labels were
transcribed from the provided reference at the displayed one-decimal precision.
No values were excluded, converted, or inferred. No replication or uncertainty
was supplied, so error bars and statistical claims are absent.

The chart inherits the established palette, white vector hatches, bold axes,
and compact single-column in-plot legend. The upper-left legend sits above the
three low-saving methods without covering their bars or labels. All eight
values are labeled with one decimal and a percent sign. Gzip on PNG retains its
true 0.2% height. Both axes and labels preserve method capitalization.

The page is 8 × 6 inches. PDF/SVG preserve editable text and vector patterns;
PNG is 2400 × 1800 px at 300 dpi and TIFF is 4800 × 3600 px at 600 dpi.
The y-axis starts at zero; no axis breaks or logarithmic transforms are used.
Reproduce with python3 plot_space_saving.py.
